Engagement Approach Templates

Privacy-safe outreach. Never mention caller name, phone, or topic. Reference timestamp only. "Our system flagged" framing. Max 1 alert/week per user.

Alert Type 1: Revenue-Stakes + No Transfer

Trigger: revenue-stakes call + no transfer configured + >1hr time-to-read.

Tier 1 (avg >24hr): "Urgent: [Company] — [X] flagged calls unreviewed. Our system shows an average [Y]-hour gap between when important calls come in and when they're seen. With call transfer, the right person on your team could've handled these live."
Tier 2 (1–24hr): "We can see you're catching up same-day, but with transfer configured, time-sensitive calls from [caller type] could reach a team member instantly."

Alert Type 2: VIP Frustration / Missing Scenario

Trigger: caller frustrated with AI, or got generic handling when a scenario should exist.

"Our system flagged a call on [day] at [time] where [AI name] applied generic handling to a caller that likely needed a more specific approach. Let's set up a caller scenario so this doesn't happen again — it's a free fix."

Volume Limit Pitch

Trigger: user at >80% of cycle limit.

"You've handled [X] calls with YouSquared — you're one of our most active users. You've hit your [limit]-call limit this cycle. With [plan], you'd get [Y] calls + [feature]."

Branch Upgrade (for All Access, ASTA, etc.)

Trigger: multiple ICs at same company already on platform.

"5 of your team members are already using YouSquared. Let's get [branch name] on a Team plan so every [equipment request / PI intake] to your branch line or your reps' direct lines is serviced instantly 24/7 — and transferred to the right person when needed."
Cadence Rules
  • Max 1 alert/week per user. Type 1 priority over Type 2.
  • Weekly digest if 3+ occurrences in a week.
  • Pause after 3 consecutive no-responses.
  • Skip if: transfer already configured, <1hr read time + scenarios exist, already on Team plan for that feature.
  • $ values are AE internal only. Never quote to user.

Principles

⏱

🎁

Free First

Always lead with free fixes (scenarios, VIPs, greeting). Paid upgrades come second.

📅

Respect the user's attention. One alert per week, pause after 3 no-responses.
